Returns & Refunds

We offer a 30-day return policy on all items. The product must be:
  • In its original condition and packaging
  • Unused and undamaged
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase

Contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order at support@eshop.com with:
  • Your order number
  • Photos of the damaged item
We'll arrange a replacement or full refund.

Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to your M-Pesa. You'll receive an SMS confirmation once the refund is sent.
